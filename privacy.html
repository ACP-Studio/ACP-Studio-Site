Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. Who we are

ACP-Studio is a personal website run by Zeyad Adel. It shows C++ projects and YouTube content. In this policy, "we" and "us" mean Zeyad Adel and ACP-Studio.

2. What we collect

  • Your email address. You type it on the log in page. It is the only personal information we ask for.
  • A sign-in time. Your browser stores the moment you logged in, together with your email, so the site remembers you.

We do not ask for your name, password, phone number, location or payment details. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs (such as your IP address and browser type) for security and to keep the site running.

3. Why we ask for your email

You need an email address to enter the site. We use it to:

  • let real people in and keep a list of the people who actually use the site, instead of anonymous automated traffic (bots);
  • know roughly how many people the site and the channel reach;
  • contact you about ACP-Studio, if we ever need to (for example, an important change to the site).

Asking for an email address makes it harder for simple automated scripts to browse and copy the site. Right now we check that the address is written correctly. We do not yet send a confirmation link, so we cannot guarantee that every address belongs to the person who typed it. We may add a confirmation step later.

We do not sell your email, rent it out, or share it for advertising. We do not send marketing emails today. If that ever changes, we will tell you first and you will be able to opt out.

4. Where your data is stored

  • In our database. Your email is saved with our database provider, Supabase, which may keep it on servers outside your country.
  • In your browser. A small item called "acpStudioUser" is saved in your browser's local storage. It expires after 30 days, and logging out removes it sooner. See our Cookies page for details.

5. Other services the site uses

The site's fonts and icons come from the site's own files, so no other company can see your visit while you browse. The only outside service the site contacts is our database provider, Supabase, at the moment you log in. Links to GitHub and YouTube take you to other websites with their own privacy policies, which we do not control.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your email for as long as the site is running or until you ask us to delete it, whichever comes first.

7. Your rights

You can ask us at any time to show you the data we hold about you, correct it, or delete it, and you can ask us to stop using your email. Email us at the address below and we will answer as soon as we can. Depending on where you live, you may have more rights under your local data protection law (for example Egypt's Personal Data Protection Law, or the GDPR in Europe).

8. Children

The site is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their email addresses. If you think a child has given us their email, contact us and we will delete it.

9. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your email, but no website can promise perfect security.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows when it last changed.
