Cookies

Last updated: November 23, 2026

1. What this page covers

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. This page explains what ACP-Studio saves on your device.

2. What we save

We do not set any tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. The site saves two items in your browser's local storage:

  • acpStudioUser: your email address and the time you logged in. It keeps you logged in, so you do not have to type your email on every page. The site cannot work without it, because login is required.
  • acpLang: your language choice (English or Arabic). The site works without it and shows English instead.

The login item stays on your device for up to 30 days, or until you log out or clear your browser data, whichever comes first. The language item stays until you clear your browser data.

3. Other services

The fonts and icons are served from this site, so browsing does not contact any other company. Two outside services are used when you log in: Supabase, the database that stores your email, and Cloudflare Turnstile, a CAPTCHA that checks you are a person. When your browser contacts them, they can see your IP address and may keep their own logs. Turnstile may also keep small items in your browser for its check. We do not control these services. Please read their own policies for details.

4. How to remove or block it

  • Use the Log out link in the menu at the top. It deletes the login item.
  • Or clear the site data in your browser settings. You will then have to log in again to use the site.
  • If you block site data completely, you will not be able to log in.
