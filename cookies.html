Cookies

Last updated: November 8, 2026

1. What this page covers

Cookies and similar technologies are small pieces of data a website saves in your browser. This page explains what ACP-Studio saves on your device.

2. What we save

We do not set any tracking, advertising or analytics cookies. The site saves one item in your browser's local storage:

  • acpStudioUser: your email address and the time you logged in. It keeps you logged in, so you do not have to type your email on every page. The site cannot work without it, because login is required.

This item stays on your device for up to 30 days, or until you log out or clear your browser data, whichever comes first.

3. Other services

The fonts and icons are served from this site, so browsing does not contact any other company. One outside service is used when you log in: Supabase, the database that stores your email. When your browser contacts it, Supabase can see your IP address and may keep its own logs. We do not control that service. Please read its own policy for details.

4. How to remove or block it

  • Use the Log out link in the menu at the top. It deletes the saved item.
  • Or clear the site data in your browser settings. You will then have to log in again to use the site.
  • If you block site data completely, you will not be able to log in.

5. Changes

If we add analytics or anything else that saves data on your device, we will update this page first.
